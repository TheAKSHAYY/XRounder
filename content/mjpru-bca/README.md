# MJP Rohilkhand University (MJPRU), Bareilly
## Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) — Complete Syllabus & Question Bank (w.e.f. 2024–25)

---

### 1. Degree Structure & Examination Scheme

- **Degree**: Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA)
- **University**: MJP Rohilkhand University, Bareilly 243006
- **Curriculum Implementation**: With effect from (w.e.f.) Session 2024–25
- **System**: Semester System with partial CBCS (Choice Based Credit System) from Semester II to VI
- **Evaluation Pattern (Theory Papers)**:
  - **Maximum Marks**: 100 per paper (30 Internal Assessment + 70 University External Exam)
  - **Internal Breakdown**: Class Interaction (5) + Quiz/Assignments (5) + Seminar/Presentation (5) + Unit Test (15) = 30 Marks
  - **Minimum Passing Marks (Theory)**: 40% (40 Marks combined)
  - **Minimum Passing Marks (Practical/Project)**: 50%
- **Summer Industrial Internship**: 6 to 8 weeks internship after Semester IV, evaluated in Semester V (BCA-307P: 100 Marks, 4 Credits).

### 3. Elective Papers Pool (E1 to E5)

1. **Elective E1 (Semester II)**:
   - `BCA-401E`: Mathematics (*Compulsory bridge course for students who didn't take Maths in 12th*)
   - `BCA-402E`: Environment and Ecology
   - `BCA-403E`: Introduction to E-Governance
2. **Elective E2 (Semester III)**:
   - `BCA-411E`: Discrete Mathematics
   - `BCA-412E`: Personality and Soft Skills Development
   - `BCA-413E`: Information System for Business
3. **Elective E3 (Semester IV)**:
   - `BCA-421E`: E-Commerce
   - `BCA-422E`: IT Acts and Cyber Laws
   - `BCA-423E`: Software Engineering
4. **Elective E4 (Semester V)**:
   - `BCA-431E`: Introduction to Cyber Security
   - `BCA-432E`: GUI Programming (.NET / Visual Basic / ASP.NET)
   - `BCA-433E`: Operation Research (LPP, Transportation, CPM/PERT)
5. **Elective E5 (Semester VI)**:
   - `BCA-441E`: Software Testing
   - `BCA-442E`: Advanced Web Development Technologies (React, Node.js, MongoDB)
   - `BCA-443E`: Blockchain Foundations (Ethereum, Solidity, Smart Contracts)
