Q1 [Unit 1] [EASY]: According to the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), what is Cloud Computing?
  (A) A physical underground storage facility for backup magnetic tapes
  (B) A model for enabling ubiquitous, convenient, on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable computing resources that can be rapidly provisioned with minimal management effort
  (C) An operating system installed exclusively on personal desktop computers
  (D) A high-speed optical fiber cable connecting continents
Correct Answer: (B)
Explanation: The official NIST Special Publication 800-145 defines cloud computing as an on-demand, ubiquitous network-accessible shared pool of configurable computing resources.

Q2 [Unit 1] [EASY]: How many essential characteristics of Cloud Computing are defined in the NIST model?
  (A) Three (3)
  (B) Four (4)
  (C) Five (5)
  (D) Seven (7)
Correct Answer: (C)
Explanation: NIST defines five essential characteristics: On-demand self-service, Broad network access, Resource pooling, Rapid elasticity, and Measured service.

Q3 [Unit 1] [EASY]: Which NIST characteristic allows a consumer to unilaterally provision computing capabilities (such as server time and network storage) automatically without human interaction with the service provider?

  (B) On-Demand Self-Service
  (C) Rapid Elasticity
  (D) Broad Network Access

Explanation: On-demand self-service permits automated provisioning of compute/storage without human intervention from the provider.

Q4 [Unit 1] [EASY]: Which NIST characteristic states that capabilities are available over the network and accessed through standard mechanisms by heterogeneous thin or thick client platforms?
